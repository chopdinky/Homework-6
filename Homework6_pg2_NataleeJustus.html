My Story

I'm a Christian, and I wanted to tell you the good news that brings me life!

Thousands of years ago, God created the world and the first two humans, Adam and Eve. And everything was very good. But then Adam and Eve sinned. And we’ve inherited that sin nature ever since. We sin against God every day by our bad thoughts or bad actions. And we see brokenness all around us. God is perfect and sinless, and He can only live with sinless people. But we are sinful. We deserve to go to hell.

But that's why Jesus came! Jesus, the Son of God, came to earth as a baby and lived a perfect, sinless life. Then, to take our punishment onto Himself, Jesus died on the cross for our sins. And three days later, He rose from the dead.

If we turn away from our sins (repent) and trust in Jesus, we will be saved! We cannot do enough good things in order to save ourselves. Even Christians will not be perfect, but God promises to help us grow! If we trust Jesus in faith, He will save us! His perfect life will be viewed as ours, and Jesus has already paid for our punishment on the cross! There are only two things we need to do to be saved: repent and trust Jesus!

John 6:40: “For this is the will of My Father, that everyone who beholds the Son and believes in Him will have eternal life, and I Myself will raise him up on the last day.”

John 3:16: “For God so loved the world, that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him shall not perish, but have eternal life.”

Romans 4:5: But to the one who does not work, but believes in Him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is credited as righteousness,
